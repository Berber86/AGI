## Rendering choices and limits
The normal/roughness maps are approximations derived from AI-generated albedos, not measured scans. Dial print is unlit for readability under a clear reflective cover. Window reflections are authored approximations, not ray tracing. Glass lenses/covers use inexpensive translucent coatings; bottle/flacon use physical transmission. This remains a compact procedural browser game, not AAA or photogrammetric asset production. Actual Android FPS unverified; SwiftShader is very slow with high-quality postprocessing and must not be used to certify mobile FPS. Raw frame timings no longer capped by animation dt.

Models are cached and statically merged by material; camera: 145 parts / 13 batches. Field geometry for horse retains only used Marching Cubes vertices. Static courtyard pauses rendering when nothing changes. Transparent decals and glass excluded from AO normal/depth to avoid false dark rectangles.
