# White Night v8 — fourth side, sky, look-up and frame budget

User (verbatim): «В Игре Белая ночь убери балконы, сделай 4 сторону двора, но с аркой и воротами. Почитай skills и постарайся увеличить fps без потери качества. Также на небе сделай звёзды и луну и соответственно угол обзора поднимающий голову к небу».

## Delivered
- Balconies deleted, not hidden (`addBalcony` removed); facades keep windows, sills, laundry, cornices.
- Fourth side closes the well: `addGateFacade` builds an extruded wall with a real arched hole, rusticated jambs, arch ring and keystone, double iron gates (one leaf ajar), arched transom, warm alley behind with a lamp, street plate and roof band. The old side arch on the back facade is gone, so the courtyard has exactly one arch — the one that was asked for.
- Sky (`src/sky.js`): static gradient dome, 1150 stars in one `Points` draw, moon sprite with craters/limb/halo. Zero per-frame work, three draw calls.
- Look-up camera: dragging in the search phase raises the head (0…60°) and turns ±41°; the bin stays tappable because hit testing uses the same camera. One-time hint, remembered in localStorage, vanishes after the first gesture.

## Performance (no item-quality change)
- Scenery tessellation is scaled by `detailScale()` (walls, mouldings, leaves, pipes); finds are built at DETAIL=1 and unchanged.
- Behind the 96% inspection veil the courtyard is not drawn at all (`static.visible=false`), restored on hide and in VR.
- Touch devices: MSAA 2× (desktop 4×), SSAO at 55% resolution. Adaptive render scale only changes pixel ratio 1…1.75 and only when the frame budget is consistently missed; materials, shadows, AO and stars are never disabled.
- Same-protocol counters: courtyard 216 → 185 calls, 423 840 → 417 878 triangles with the new fourth wall; inspection 173 → 58 calls, 365 952 → 188 454 triangles. Low quality courtyard 81 → 72 calls.

## Limits
Physical Android FPS/heat, a real finger swipe and the adaptive scale on hardware are untested; the look gesture is verified through the test bridge and pointer emulation. Sky is an artistic white-night scene, not an astronomical chart. No PR opened; branch pushed only.
